Year-Round Roadway Dining Means Year-Round Signage

Before the law changed, most roadside signage was built to last one season and come down with the shed. Now that roadway cafés can stay up, their menu boards, panels, and directionals need to be specified the same way — for salt spray, freeze-thaw cycling, and low winter light, not just a summer patio.

Outdoor Menu Boards

Weatherproof, backlit or matte-laminated boards sized for curbside sheds and parklets, swappable for seasonal menus.

Branded Panels

Structural fascia and railing panels — your logo and colors built into the dining enclosure itself, not stuck on after.

Directional Signage

Sidewalk-to-seating wayfinding: host stand markers, "enter here" flags, and curb-to-door arrows for narrow storefronts.

Winter-Rated Panels

Freeze-cycle-tested aluminum and HDPE substrates that won't warp, fade, or crack under rock salt and plow spray.

Window & Door Decals

Cut-vinyl hours, specials, and heater notices for storefront glass — removable without leaving adhesive scarring.

Sandwich Boards & A-Frames

Ballasted A-frames rated for open avenues and wind tunnels between buildings, with quick-change chalk or magnetic inserts.

Awning Graphics

Screen-printed or digitally imaged awning skins, refreshed on the same frame you already have permitted with the city.

Heater & Safety Signage

Required clearance, propane, and fire-code postings, designed so compliance signage doesn't clash with your branding.

String Light & Canopy Branding

Custom pennants, canopy valances, and lit signage that carry your identity after dark, when roadside dining draws its crowd.
