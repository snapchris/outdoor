Built For Local Law 158

Signage rated for winter, not dismantled for it.

Before the year-round law, roadside signage was designed to last one season. We build the opposite — substrates and finishes chosen for salt spray, freeze-thaw cycling, and low winter light, so the same signage that draws a summer crowd still reads clearly under a streetlight in February.

How We Work

From sidewalk survey to finished install.

01

Site Survey

We measure your shed, parklet, or storefront, and confirm DOT and DOB limits before design begins.

02

Brand & Layout

Menu boards, panels, and directionals are laid out together, so the set reads as one identity.

03

Fabrication

Materials are specified for the block you occupy — wind, salt spray, and sun angle all inform the build.

04

Install & Check

We install on your structure, and return before the first freeze to confirm mounts and seals hold.
